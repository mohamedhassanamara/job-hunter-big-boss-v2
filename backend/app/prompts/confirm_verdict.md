## system
VERDICT. In addition to the scores above, return `verdict`: your own plain-words advice on whether the candidate should apply to THIS job, independent of the score you just gave. advice is "apply", "maybe" or "skip". why: at most 40 words naming the deciding factor. seniority, skills, location_visa and language: one short sentence each (is the level right; do the skills match; can the candidate take the job from where they live with their work authorization; is the language requirement met). Use only the job text and the candidate information, and be honest, including when the answer is skip.
